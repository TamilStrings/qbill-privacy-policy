Privacy Policy for QBill

Effective Date: November 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy governs your use of the QBill mobile application created by QBill. We respect your privacy and are committed to ensuring your personal and financial data remains secure.

1. Data Collection and Usage

QBill does not collect, track, or share any personal data, usage metrics, or billing records.

  • All invoices, receipts, expenses, and billing data generated within QBill are stored locally on your device's internal storage.
  • We operate no central analytics servers, ad trackers, or third-party behavioral profiling tools.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade any user information to third parties.

2. Optional Google Drive Integration

QBill provides an optional backup and restore feature powered by Google Drive:

  • OAuth Authentication: If you choose to connect your Google account, authentication occurs directly through Google's official sign-in services. QBill never receives, handles, or stores your Google account password.
  • Scope of Access: QBill requests restricted access solely to its own dedicated application data folder or selected backup folder on your Google Drive (drive.appdata or drive.file scope). The app cannot read, modify, or delete any other files stored in your Drive.
  • Data Transmission: When you initiate a backup or restore, your data is transmitted directly and encrypted between your device and Google's servers. No backup data passes through any developer-owned or intermediary servers.

3. Device Permissions

QBill may request specific Android system permissions strictly for core functionality:

  • Internet: Required exclusively to communicate with Google Drive APIs during user-initiated backups or sync actions.
  • Storage / Media: Required only if you choose to export PDF/Excel invoices directly to your device's file storage.

4. Third-Party Services

QBill relies only on Google APIs for authentication and cloud backup. Their privacy practices are governed by Google's own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy

5. Data Deletion and Retention

Because your billing data is stored locally on your device, deleting the application permanently removes all local records. If you utilized Google Drive backup, you can delete your backup file directly from your Google Drive account or revoke QBill's access at any time through your Google Account Security settings.
